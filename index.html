Services

What we take care of

If it has to do with your yard, we do it. Every job is priced and measured in units you can check, so the quote matches the work.

Lawn mowing and edging

Per visit, weekly or every other week

Mowed, edged along walks and beds, and blown clean before we leave.

Mulch and bed care

By the cubic yard

Weeding, edging and fresh mulch to keep beds tidy and hold moisture.

Planting and installs

By the plant or by the bed

Shrubs, trees, perennials and new beds, placed for the sun and space you have.

Sod and seeding

By the square foot

Fix bare patches or start a new lawn, with grading and prep done first.

Patios and walkways

By the square foot

Pavers and stone laid on a compacted base so they stay level.

Trimming and pruning

By the job or by the hour

Hedges, shrubs and small trees shaped and cleaned up.

Leaf removal

By the job

Leaves cleared from lawns and beds in fall and hauled away.

Snow plowing

By the visit

Driveways and lots plowed so you can get out after a storm.

Snow shoveling

By the visit

Walkways, steps and entrances shoveled by hand.
